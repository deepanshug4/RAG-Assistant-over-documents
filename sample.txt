ACME LOGISTICS — EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK (2026)

1. WORKING HOURS
Standard working hours are 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday to Friday.
Employees are entitled to a one-hour lunch break. Core collaboration
hours, during which all employees must be available, are 11:00 AM to
4:00 PM. Remote work is permitted up to three days per week with manager
approval.

2. LEAVE POLICY
Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per year.
Annual leave must be requested at least two weeks in advance. Unused
leave of up to 5 days may be carried over into the next calendar year.
Sick leave is capped at 12 days per year and requires a medical
certificate for absences longer than three consecutive days.

3. EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT
Business expenses must be submitted within 30 days of the expense date.
Meals during business travel are reimbursed up to 40 USD per day.
Any single expense above 500 USD requires prior written approval from a
department head. Reimbursements are processed on the 15th of each month.

4. EQUIPMENT POLICY
Each employee is issued a company laptop and is responsible for its
safekeeping. Lost or damaged equipment must be reported to IT within
24 hours. Personal use of company equipment should be kept to a minimum.

5. CODE OF CONDUCT
Employees must treat colleagues with respect. Harassment of any kind is
strictly prohibited and may result in immediate termination. Confidential
company information must not be shared outside the organization.

6. PERFORMANCE REVIEWS
Formal performance reviews are conducted twice per year, in June and
December. Promotions are considered during the December review cycle only.
